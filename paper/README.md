# paper/

Journal manuscript and the assets it uses. Files here are frozen copies produced
by the scripts in `uq/`; regenerate them there rather than editing here.

- `manuscript/` — LaTeX source (elsarticle): `main.tex`, `refs.bib`,
  `sections/` (see `manuscript/README.md`)
- `figures/` — publication figures (PNG + PDF), generated by
  `python -m uq.figures`
- `tables/` — publication tables (CSV), generated by `python -m uq.tables`
- `data/` — frozen copies of the datasets behind the manuscript numbers
  (DOE dataset, optimization fronts, knee designs, Monte-Carlo verification
  runs, dispersion probe, design-space scan)
- `supplementary/` — supplementary figure (NSGA-II convergence)
- `thesis_assets/` — figures extracted from the thesis document, for restyling

Phase-1 validation material is in `validation/` and `results/` at the repo
root. Plan and decision log: `uq/PLAN.md`.
